## 1. Executive Summary
Implemented and verified all requirements in [`functional_specification_113.md`](file:///Users/diesel/desktop/bartending/Bartending/functional_specification_113.md):
- **Non-Occluding 2-Slide Patron Inspection Carousel** (`[data-testid="patron-inspection-carousel"]`): Clicking a seated patron `x` isolates patron `x`, binds their order to the bar mat, and opens the 2-slide carousel on the opposite horizontal half of the Game Boy glass (`bar_seat_1`/`bar_seat_2` → right `48%–96%` X; `bar_seat_3`/`bar_seat_4` → left `4%–52%` X) so patron `x`'s seated sprite is never covered.
- **Bidirectional Carousel Switching**: Players can switch between Slide 1 (**Order Receipt**, `[data-testid="carousel-slide-order"]`) and Slide 2 (**Conversation Box**, `[data-testid="patron-conversation-box"]`) via on-screen controls (`◀`, `ORDER`, `CHAT`, `▶`), Game Boy `D-Pad` (`Left`/`Right`), and keyboard arrow keys (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`).
- **Full-Screen Patron Chatbox (`96% × 96%`)**: Engaging in conversation expands the Conversation Box into `[data-testid="patron-chatbox-fullscreen"]` covering `96.0% × 96.0%` of the playable Game Boy screen (`--shell-glass-w` × `--shell-glass-h`), styled in the Retro RPG Dialogue theme (`#09133b` navy, dual white/black pixel borders, 4 SVG corner loops).
- **Patron Portrait & Display Name**: Displays the patron's portrait (`[data-testid="chatbox-patron-photo"]`) inside a silver beveled frame alongside their canonical display name (`[data-testid="chatbox-patron-name"]`) in gold pixel typography (`#f7ca18`).
- **Live LLM Conversation Starters & Interactive Chat**: Extended `POST /api/dialogue` (`src/app/api/dialogue/route.ts`, `src/lib/hfDialogueService.ts`, `src/data/characterDialogue.ts`) with `type: 'starters'` and `type: 'chat'`, grounded in each character's authoritative `personality.txt` / persona record and enforcing fail-fast error handling (`PersonaNotFoundError` 404, 400 validation, 502 upstream error banner `[data-testid="patron-chat-error"]`).
